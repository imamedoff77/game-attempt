## Full description (≤4000)
Build a base. Train an army. Outsmart your rival.

Iron Doctrine is a real-time strategy game made for your phone. Gather your forces, manage your power and supplies, and lead your troops across a desert battlefield. Every match is a head-to-head duel of planning, timing and nerve.

BUILD YOUR BASE
Raise power plants, supply centers, barracks and vehicle factories. Keep the lights on: a base without enough energy stops working. Upgrade your headquarters to unlock a bigger army and stronger units.

COMMAND YOUR ARMY
Riflemen, rocket troopers, snipers, jeeps, armored cars and tanks, each with its own strengths. Select a squad with a tap or draw a box, then move, attack, hold position, or attack-move and let them fight their way across the map. See your orders drawn on the ground and keep an eye on the minimap.

THREE WAYS TO PLAY
- Campaign: ten missions with star goals for speed and losses.
- Play against the computer on Easy, Medium or Hard.
- Online 1v1: ranked matches against players of your level, or a private room to play a friend with a code.

GROW STRONGER
Earn credits by winning and spend them in the Armory on permanent upgrades for every unit type. Come back daily for login rewards and tasks, and keep your streak alive.

FAIR AND SIMPLE
- Free to play, no ads.
- Ranked matches are played without Armory upgrades, so everybody starts equal.
- Optional purchases: credit packs and a double-credits booster.
- Your progress is tied to your account and follows you to a new phone.

Fast to learn, deep to master. Show your rival what a good doctrine can do.
