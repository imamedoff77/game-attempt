## What we collect and why

| Data | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| E-mail address, display name | to create and recover your account | our account server |
| Password (stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash, never in readable form) | to sign you in | our account server |
| Google account identifier and e-mail (only if you choose "Sign in with Google") | to sign you in | our account server |
| Game progress: campaign stars, credits, armory levels, daily rewards, tutorial state, lifetime statistics | to keep your progress when you change phone | our account server and your phone |
| Online rating, number of ranked matches and wins | to match you with similar players | our account server |
| Match commands of online matches (what players ordered, no voice, no text) | to run the match, check the winner and detect cheating | our game server, kept only while needed for the result |
| Quick chat phrases (fixed phrases, no free text) | in-match communication | passed to your opponent, not stored |
| Purchase records (product, Google order id, purchase token, time) | to deliver what you bought and prevent double delivery | our account server |
| IP address and technical logs | security, abuse prevention | our servers, short retention [N days] |

We do **not** show advertising, do not sell your data, and do not collect precise location, contacts, photos or microphone input. The game does not track you across other apps.

## Your choices and rights

- **Delete your account and its data** inside the game: Account → Delete account (needs your password); or write to [contact e-mail]. Deleting removes your profile, rating and sessions; purchase records may be kept in anonymous form where the law requires.
- You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or object to processing by writing to [contact e-mail]. If you live in the EU/EEA/UK you can also complain to your data protection authority.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 where local law says so) and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

## Security

Traffic to our servers uses HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, session tokens are stored only as hashes, purchases and match results are checked on the server. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will tell you as the law requires.
